# ChatGPT Research Clusters

This folder reorganizes the long-form research generated in ChatGPT conversations: theses, monographs, textbooks, implementation handbooks, architecture reports, and their PDF exports. It is deliberately separate from the raw transcript notes and generated artifact directories.

## Research clusters

- [[Transcripts/Research/01 - Markdown Thesis and PDF Inventory|Markdown Thesis and PDF Inventory]] — file-level register of generated Markdown and PDF artifacts.
- [[Transcripts/Research/02 - PBUI and CLIM Research|PBUI and CLIM Research]] — presentation-based UI, semantic interfaces, widget DSLs, CLIM, Sentinel, typed selectors, and the P01–P15 research program.
- [[Transcripts/Research/10 - PBUI-MATHS Pattern Zoo Handbook|PBUI-MATHS Pattern Zoo Handbook]] — fourteen recurring semantic-interface patterns with first-week explanations, formal semantics, advanced mathematics, worked examples, alias maps, and exact source sightings.
- [[Transcripts/Research/03 - RAG and Retrieval Research|RAG and Retrieval Research]] — rag-ttc, compositional retrieval, semantic provenance, durable boundaries, and RAG job systems.
- [[Transcripts/Research/09 - RAG-MATHS Pattern Zoo|RAG-MATHS Pattern Zoo]] — a first-week-friendly textbook of twelve recurring semantic patterns, each paired with its category-theoretic or abstract-mathematical formulation and source sightings.
- [[Transcripts/Research/04 - Mathematics and Formal Foundations|Mathematics and Formal Foundations]] — structural mathematics editors, category/type theory, Lean/HoTT, ERP ontologies, and formal semantics.
- [[Transcripts/Research/05 - CNC and CAM Research|CNC and CAM Research]] — CAM algorithms, Dropcut, CNC IR design, G-code semantics, and Z1 manufacturing workflows.
- [[Transcripts/Research/06 - Goja, Interpreters, and Language Systems|Goja, Interpreters, and Language Systems]] — tiny-idp, malleable interpreters, Langblocks, instrumentation, and hosted Goja.
- [[Transcripts/Research/07 - Go-Go-WM and Semantic Desktop Research|Go-Go-WM and Semantic Desktop Research]] — programmable presentation environments, window-manager architecture, and semantic desktop design.
- [[Transcripts/Research/08 - Research Operations, DataDrop, and AI Assurance|Research Operations, DataDrop, and AI Assurance]] — DataDrop, scraper workflows, researchctl operations, agent systems, and fractal assurance.

## How to use this folder

1. Start with the cluster note for a material-level synthesis.
2. Follow the source-conversation links to inspect the original request and dialogue.
3. Follow the Markdown/PDF links for the generated deliverables.
4. Treat repeated filenames as revisions or branches, not automatically as identical documents.

## Provenance and caveats

- These notes are navigational research summaries over generated artifacts; they do not replace the original transcripts.
- The archive contains regenerated branches and duplicate filenames. Links are path-qualified where possible.
- Some PDFs are exports of Markdown documents; both are listed when present.
- The original archive did not preserve ChatGPT Project IDs, so these are topic clusters rather than authoritative Project inventories.
