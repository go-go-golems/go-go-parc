We covered up

It’s much more practical

The heart does not dare  
Yet inside each other  
Life goes on its way  
Brushing against the wind, Saint Vincent  

We’re not nihilists  
It’s the street saying all of that

Is you—you’ll tell me all about it

Three little bullets in the back  
Saint Vincent plays with words

One little stab of the knife

Saint Vincent plays with words
