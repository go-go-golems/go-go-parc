For a first interview, choose **four to six** of these:

### Strong opening questions

1. **What is the most important problem you want this person to solve in the first six months?**

2. **What would make you say, after 90 days, that you hired the right person?**

3. **Why is Matterhaul hiring for this role now?**

4. **What qualities tend to separate people who succeed at Matterhaul from those who would struggle here?**

### Questions tailored to Matterhaul

5. **Which customer workflow is currently the highest priority—quoting, ordering, procurement, dispatch, or something else?**

6. **What have you learned from distributors and manufacturers that most surprised the team?**

7. **Where do customers currently experience the most friction when adopting Matterhaul?**

8. **How do you decide which customer requests should shape the product and which are too specific to one customer?**

9. **What does Matterhaul need to prove over the next 12 months?**

10. **What is the largest risk facing the company right now: product adoption, technical execution, sales, or something else?**

These are especially relevant because Matterhaul describes itself as a small, recently founded company serving physical supply-chain businesses, where employees are likely to have unusually broad ownership. citeturn571143search1turn571143search6

### Role and working-style questions

11. **What decisions would I be expected to make independently?**

12. **How are priorities set when several customer or product needs are urgent?**

13. **Who would I work with most closely, and how would we divide responsibilities?**

14. **Could you describe a recent difficult decision the team made and how it was resolved?**

15. **How much of this role is executing an existing plan versus helping define the plan?**

16. **What does feedback look like on such a small team?**

17. **What does the onsite working environment look like in practice?**

### Good closing questions

18. **Is there anything in my background that you would like me to clarify before we finish?**

19. **What are the remaining stages of the interview process, and what will each stage evaluate?**

20. **Based on our conversation, what should I study or prepare for the next interview?**

A particularly strong set would be **1, 5, 8, 11, 14, and 18**. Avoid asking all 20; it can make the conversation feel scripted.
