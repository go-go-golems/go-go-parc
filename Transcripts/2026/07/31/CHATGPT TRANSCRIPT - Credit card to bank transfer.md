> How can I transfer money from credit card onto my bank account

You generally can't make a standard bank transfer directly from a credit card to your bank account, but there are several ways to move funds. The available options depend on your card issuer.

The most common methods are:

- **Cash advance:** Many credit cards let you withdraw cash from an ATM or transfer cash advances to your bank account. This is usually the fastest option, but it's also the most expensive. Cash advances often have:
  - An upfront fee (commonly 3–5% of the amount).
  - A higher interest rate than regular purchases.
  - Interest that starts accruing immediately (no grace period).

- **Balance transfer checks or deposit offers:** Some card issuers send convenience checks or offer promotions that let you transfer funds into a checking account. These may have lower promotional rates, but they usually charge a balance transfer fee.

- **Money transfer through your card issuer:** Some issuers allow you to request a direct transfer from your credit card to your bank account through their website or app. If available, this is typically treated as a cash advance or balance transfer.

Before transferring money, check:
- The cash advance fee and APR.
- Your available cash advance limit (which is often lower than your total credit limit).
- Whether there are any promotional transfer offers.

If your goal is simply to get cash into your bank account, a personal loan or line of credit may be significantly less expensive than a credit card cash advance.
